Delete Your Scrub Prep Account

Scrub Prep, by Bender Apps, LLC. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Scrub Prep and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Can't open the app?

Email support@benderapps.dev with the subject "Delete my Scrub Prep account", from the email address you signed up with. We'll delete your account and confirm by reply, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address (or Apple ID identifier).
  • Saved cases and the notes attached to them.
  • Quiz Me sessions and Rapid Fire history.
  • Usage records tied to your account.

What is kept

Nothing tied to your account is retained after deletion. Subscription billing records are held by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), not by us, and are governed by their policies. Cancel your subscription through the App Store or Google Play separately; deleting your account does not cancel it.

Delete some data only

To keep your account, delete any saved case or Quiz Me session in the app, or clear a case's notes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
