Educational Use Disclaimer

Last updated September 17, 2026

Scrub Prep is a study and rehearsal tool for medical students. It is not a source of medical advice or patient-care guidance.

Educational Purposes Only

Scrub Prep is designed to help medical students prepare for the surgery clerkship — reviewing anatomy, operative steps, and likely questions before a case. It is a study aid, not a clinical resource, and is not intended for use during patient care.

No Medical Advice

The content provided in this app does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations, and is not a substitute for the guidance of a qualified clinician. Clinical decisions should always be made under the supervision of your attending physician and clinical team.

AI-Generated Content

Case preparations, questions, and feedback in Scrub Prep are generated by an AI language model. AI-generated content can be incomplete, outdated, or simply wrong. Always verify important information against your institution's guidelines, your clinical team, and primary medical literature.

No Guarantee of Accuracy or Exam Performance

We do not guarantee that Scrub Prep's content is accurate, complete, or current, or that using the app will improve your performance on any exam, evaluation, or clinical rotation.

User Responsibility

You are responsible for verifying any information before relying on it and for never entering real patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, or other identifying information into the app. Use of Scrub Prep is at your own discretion and risk.
